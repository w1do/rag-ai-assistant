# Typography — ManuFlex

## Font Families
- **Headings:** `DM Serif Display`
- **Body & Interface:** `DM Sans`

## Type Scales (Desktop)
| Name | Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking |
|------|------|-------------|--------|----------|
| `Display` | 56px | 1.08 | Bold | -0.03em |
| `Title` | 40px | 1.15 | Bold | -0.02em |
| `Heading` | 28px | 1.25 | Semibold | -0.01em |
| `Subheading` | 18px | 1.4 | Medium | 0 |
| `Body` | 15px | 1.6 | Regular | 0.01em |
| `Small` | 13px | 1.5 | Regular | 0.01em |
| `Caption` | 11px | 1.4 | Medium | 0.02em |

## Responsive Scaling (Mobile)
- **Display:** scales down from 56px to **32px**.
- **Title:** scales down from 40px to **26px**.
- **Body:** remains consistent at **15px**.

## Styling Principles
- **Editorial Feel:** The combination of a serif heading font with a clean sans-serif body creates a premium industrial editorial feel.
- **Authority:** Bold serif headings project precision and technological sophistication.
