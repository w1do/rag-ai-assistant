# Layout & Composition — ManuFlex

## Layout System
- **Grid:** 12-column grid on desktop.
- **Margins:** ~80px side margins on desktop.
- **Composition:** Asymmetric split layouts (large imagery vs. text).
- **Vertical Spacing:** Generous whitespace between sections (`Section gap: 80px`).

## Content Organization
- **Hierarchical Spacing:** Use `3XL` (120px) for hero areas and `2XL` (80px) for standard section gaps.
- **Asymmetry:** Contrast large-scale photography with clean, structured text columns.
- **Editorial Feel:** Maintain high whitespace ratios to emphasize industrial precision.

## Decorative Patterns
- **Brand Wordmark:** Large bold brand wordmark ('MANUFLEX') used as a decorative typographic background element behind hero content.
- **3D Isometric Icons:** Illustrations for machinery and industrial equipment (robotic arms, CNC machines) used in feature grids.
- **Glassmorphic Stat Overlays:** Floating metric displays on hero imagery with circular progress indicators.
