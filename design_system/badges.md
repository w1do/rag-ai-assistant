# Badges & Tags — ManuFlex

## Badge Specifications

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Shape** | Pill-shaped (Rounded-full) |
| **Radius** | 9999px (`--radius-full`) |
| **Font** | `DM Sans`, 11px-13px (`--text-caption` or `--text-small`) |
| **Weight** | Medium |

## Variants

### 1. Solid Badge
- **Background:** `#111111` (Light mode) or `#F0F0F0` (Dark mode).
- **Text:** Contrasting white or dark text.
- **Usage:** Standard category labels.

### 2. Glassmorphic Badge (Image Overlay)
- **Background:** Semi-transparent with backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Text:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Usage:** Floating on photography (e.g., 'Efficiency', '80% Automated').

### 3. Industrial Accent
- **Background:** `#C8A645` (Gold/Industrial accent).
- **Text:** `#FFFFFF`.
- **Usage:** Highlights, special status.

## Placement
- **Card Overlays:** Positioned as floating editorial elements on imagery.
- **Hero Stats:** Often paired with circular progress indicators in glassmorphic stat overlays.
